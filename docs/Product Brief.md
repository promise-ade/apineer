### **Product Name** 

Apineer

### **Problem**

Developers and product teams have a hard time choosing the right fintech APIs for their products. From going through multiple documentation, asking for recommendations from peers, comparing pricing across providers and discovering lesser-known but effective APIs only after others have been implemented, it’s a struggle that leaves teams spending more time researching APIs than building the products their users actually care about. 

Developers and product teams sometimes struggle to choose the right fintech API for their products. They spend hours going through multiple documentation, relying on peer recommendations, comparing pricing across providers and even discover better APIs after committing to an integration.

### **Vision**

To make it easy for developers and product teams to find solutions they can easily trust

### **Solution**

A platform to easily discover, evaluate and compare APIs offered by different fintech providers

### **Users**

Developers  
Product teams  
Startup founders
